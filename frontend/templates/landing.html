Frequently asked questions

Is ScholrNet free?
Yes. ScholrNet is completely free for students. Always. No hidden charges, no premium tiers.
How does achievement verification work?
Your school counselor or teacher confirms your achievements through their dashboard. It takes 2 seconds. Once verified, your profile shows a trust badge that colleges and employers recognize.
What if my school isn't on ScholrNet?
You can request your school to join, or ask any teacher on the platform to verify your achievements. We're adding new schools every week.
Can colleges see my profile?
Yes. Your profile generates a shareable verification link. Send it with college applications, scholarship forms, or internship resumes. It's like a resume that can't lie.
Is my data private?
Your profile is only visible to connections you approve. Nothing is public by default except posts you choose to share on the public timeline. You control what's visible.
I'm a teacher. Can I verify students?
Yes. Sign up as a teacher or school counselor and you'll get a verification dashboard. You can confirm student achievements and post opportunities for your school.
