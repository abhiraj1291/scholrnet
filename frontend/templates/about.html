About Us

Building the academic
trust network for students

ScholrNet is a student-built platform that helps ambitious students discover opportunities and build verified academic profiles — all in one place.

Our Story

It started with a simple observation

Students were missing opportunities — not because they weren't qualified, but because the opportunities were scattered across dozens of websites, WhatsApp groups, and social media channels. A scholarship deadline passed while a student was studying for exams. An internship application closed before anyone even knew it existed.

At the same time, students were building impressive portfolios — winning competitions, leading projects, earning certifications — but had no way to present them credibly. A college application or internship resume listed achievements with no proof behind them.

ScholrNet was built to solve both problems: a single place to find opportunities, and a verified profile that makes every achievement count.

One platform. Opportunities you'd otherwise miss. A profile that opens doors.

Why We Built It

Opportunities should find the right students

The internet has more opportunities than ever — online courses, global competitions, research programs, startup internships, scholarships. But they're buried in newsletters, Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, and college notice boards.

Most students don't have time to hunt across platforms. Many don't even know what's available to them. And when they do find something, they often hesitate to apply because their profile doesn't stand out.

ScholrNet aggregates opportunities in one feed, tailored to students. And it gives every student a credible, verifiable profile — so your application speaks for itself.

Our Mission & Vision

Every ambitious student deserves
a fair shot at the future

We're building the infrastructure for student opportunity — making it accessible, credible, and connected.

Mission

To give every student — regardless of school, background, or location — equal access to opportunities and a credible way to showcase their achievements.

Vision

A world where a student's potential is measured by what they've accomplished, not where they come from — and where every achievement opens the next door.

How We Do It

We aggregate internships, scholarships, hackathons, competitions, research programs, and clubs into one platform — and give every student a school-verified profile they own.

Who It's For

Ambitious students in high school and early college who want to build real credentials, explore career paths, and stand out in competitive applications.

Meet the Founder

Built by a student, for students

ScholrNet was started by Abhiraj, a high school student who saw the gap between talent and opportunity — and decided to build the bridge.

Abhiraj
Founder, ScholrNet

Abhiraj is a high school student studying in Class 10 with a deep interest in technology, startups, and education. He built ScholrNet after noticing that talented students around him were missing life-changing opportunities — not because they lacked ability, but because the ecosystem was fragmented.

He started coding at a young age and quickly realized that software could solve real problems. ScholrNet is his attempt to use technology to level the playing field for students across India and beyond.

Abhiraj believes that a student's background should never determine their ceiling. He continues to build ScholrNet while balancing school, learning about product development, and talking to users to understand what they need.

"I started ScholrNet because I saw students miss deadlines, lose opportunities, and struggle to prove what they'd accomplished. That shouldn't happen. The internet gives us the tools to fix it — so I built something."

— Abhiraj, Founder

What drives this project

The problem is real
Thousands of students miss opportunities every year because they simply don't know they exist. ScholrNet makes discovery effortless.
Credentials need to be credible
Any student can list achievements. ScholrNet's school verification system turns claims into credentials that colleges and employers can trust.
Building in public
ScholrNet is being built openly, with real users and real feedback. Every feature comes from talking to students, teachers, and schools.
